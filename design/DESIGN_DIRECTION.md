# Approved visual and interaction direction

## What the owner approved

A Warcraft-inspired illustrated chronicle in composition and typography, with a practical field-reference area. The initial Illustrated Chronicle / Adventurer's Field Journal concept was refined through feedback: the artificial journal was rejected and removed. Do not restore it merely because it appears in older concepts.

- Warm parchment as the default; dark stone as an alternative.
- Elegant, exceptionally readable long-form type. Local bundled fonts; comfortable measure, spacing and text-size controls.
- Lore chapters feel composed and atmospheric: restrained gold rules, deep forest-green chapter banners, moon ornament, generous reading space and drop caps.
- Atmosphere should come from typography, composition and restrained CSS details. No generated imagery, paid illustration workflow or runtime AI dependency. The owner preferred removal of the images.
- On desktop, every lore chapter keeps a chapter list and an outline of the visible headings. The mobile layout may adapt; do not force a desktop panel onto a phone.
- Class guidance and troubleshooting use practical, scannable layouts and ordinary Markdown tables/lists rather than decorative lore treatment.

## Navigation and spoilers

Normal page changes are welcome. Terms link to dedicated glossary pages, and Back/Back to your passage returns to the reading location. Popups and overlays were strongly disliked, especially blackout backdrops, sudden appearances and unexpected scrolling.

Glossary and chapter passages reveal only the manually selected story milestones. Apply the same boundaries to entry pages, glossary lists, outlines and search. This is local casual spoiler filtering, not secure access control. No game synchronization or automatic quest detection.

## Reference and scope

`reference-shadowglen.jpg` captures v0.2 with a persistent chapter panel on Among the boughs. It is visual reference only, not an asset to display inside the site. The source CSS/components are the more complete reference for both themes and practical layouts.

Preserve the current design during the first external-content pass. Refine particular issues if observed; do not start another broad redesign or add artwork for completeness.
