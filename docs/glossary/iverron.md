## Why Dirania needs your help

A spider bite has left Iverron poisoned. Dirania knows a remedy, but needs mushrooms, lilies and Webwood ichor to prepare it.

The ingredients you gather belong to this practical request. Bringing them back gives Dirania what she needs to make the antidote.
