## Why it matters in Ban’ethil

The druids’ bodies remain in the den while their spirits are away. They depend on the sanctuary being kept safe, and the Gnarlpine have taken that protection from them.

The relics you have been asked to recover are meant to awaken the druids. Their work in the Dream does not make their sleeping bodies safe from danger in the waking world.
