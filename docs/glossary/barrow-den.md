## Why it matters in Ban’ethil

Ban’ethil shelters living druids. Their bodies remain in its chambers while their spirits travel the [Emerald Dream](./emerald-dream.md).

The Gnarlpine have occupied the den, leaving those sleeping bodies vulnerable. Its role as a sanctuary is the reason Athridas is alarmed by Gaerolas’s report.
