## Why they matter in Ban’ethil

The Gnarlpine have occupied Ban’ethil and threaten the druids sleeping there. Recovering the relics takes your party into the chambers they now hold.

What you learn about this group is part of their story in Teldrassil. It does not establish the history of every furbolg you might meet.
