## Beneath Aldrassil

Your journey begins in Shadowglen, beneath Aldrassil. Thaloren travels with Darethia, Lunethra, Faelion and Saerith; the people you meet here give the party its first reasons to venture into the surrounding woods.

The work is local and personal. Before the road carries you farther through Teldrassil, there are people nearby who need help.

<StoryGate milestone="diranias-request">

---

## An antidote for Iverron

Dirania has warned [Iverron](../glossary/iverron.md) about those spiders before. Now a bite has left him poisoned, and she needs your help to prepare an antidote.

Gather mushrooms and lilies nearby, along with Webwood ichor from the same spiders that poisoned him. Bring the ingredients back so she can make the remedy.

</StoryGate>

---

## The road beyond Shadowglen

When your work here is finished, the road leads toward Dolanaar. That next part of the journey brings the party to the people looking after Teldrassil’s moonwells and the druids sleeping in Ban’ethil.
