## Beneath the roots

Ban’ethil is a place of sleep, but the druids resting here are not simply waiting to wake. Their spirits travel the [Emerald Dream](./glossary/emerald-dream.md) while their bodies remain in the barrow den.

That separation leaves them vulnerable. The [Gnarlpine](./glossary/gnarlpine.md) have taken over the den, and the sleeping druids can no longer rely on the sanctuary around them.

---

## The relics of wakening

Gaerolas’s report has confirmed Athridas Bearmantle’s fears: the druids in Ban’ethil are in danger. Waking them requires four sacred objects—the Raven Claw Talisman, the Black Feather Quill, the Sapphire of Sky and the Rune of Nesting.

Athridas has asked you to recover these relics from the den. The druids’ spirits may be far from Ban’ethil, but their bodies are still here, exposed while the Gnarlpine hold the chambers around them.

## A body left behind

[Oben](./glossary/oben-rageclaw.md) has examined the charm you brought him. He believes it can free him from the Gnarlpine, but first he needs you to kill his body. You have been speaking to his spirit; his physical form remains under their control, deeper in the den.

He asks you to find it, use the charm on the body after the fight, and return to him.
