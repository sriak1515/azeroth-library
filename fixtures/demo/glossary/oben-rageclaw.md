## The druid you have met

The spirit speaking to you is Oben Rageclaw. He needs your help with the Gnarlpine in the den.

<StoryGate chapter="kal-02-banethil" milestone="charm-examined">

After examining the charm, Oben explains that his physical body remains under the Gnarlpine’s control. He believes the charm can free him, but asks you first to kill his body, use the charm on its remains, and return to him.

</StoryGate>
