## The landscape of your first chapters

Thaloren’s journey began in Shadowglen. The road has since taken your party to Dolanaar and the sleeping druids of Ban’ethil.

These places belong to the same forest home. The trouble inside the den is one of the concerns you encounter beyond Shadowglen.
