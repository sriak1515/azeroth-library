## AltBot shows “0 bots”

1. **Check the mode first.** In your earlier session, the board was in Solo mode. Switching to Quest mode made the party appear.
2. **Check the party.** Make sure your companions are still grouped with Thaloren and nearby.
3. **Reopen the board.** If it still shows no bots, note the active mode and what the board displays before changing other settings.

## The guide arrow is unhelpful inside Ban’ethil

The den has multiple levels. A map arrow alone may not identify the right passage. Use the guide’s interior landmarks and objective text for the current step.

Check the current objective before advancing manually. Moving the guide forward does not complete the quest.

## A companion’s bags are full

Open that companion’s inventory in AltBot to see what is taking space. At a vendor, use the addon’s sell controls to clear unwanted items.

Away from a vendor, inspect the inventory first and choose what can be discarded. Keep quest items.

## Questie disappears while TomTom is enabled

Your latest playtest identified a clash between Questie and TomTom. If that symptom returns, disable TomTom and reload the interface to check whether Questie returns before changing Questie’s settings.
