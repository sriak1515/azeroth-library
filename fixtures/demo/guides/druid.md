## Your everyday rhythm

Let Darethia engage. On ordinary enemies, cast **Wrath**, then another only if necessary. Faelion and Saerith may finish the target before your second cast.

**Moonfire** is useful when moving, tagging, or facing an enemy that will live long enough for its damage over time to matter. You do not need to apply it to every target.

| Situation | A useful response |
|---|---|
| Ordinary enemy | Wrath; another only when needed. |
| Several enemies | Focus the same target Darethia is attacking. |
| A sturdier named enemy | Let Darethia establish control; add Moonfire if it will have time to work. |
| A stray enemy reaches you | Use Entangling Roots or Nature’s Grasp if learned, then reposition. |
| Lunethra needs help | Rejuvenation offers a small safety net; Healing Touch is a larger, slower rescue. |

Use the spells you have actually learned. **Bear Form** becomes an emergency option after you have earned it through your class quest; Darethia remains the party’s tank.

## Your first talent points

This follows the early portion of your existing playbook. Spend each new point at the corresponding level.

| Level | New point | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Starlight Wrath 1/5 | Shorter Wrath cast. |
| 11 | Starlight Wrath 2/5 | Faster casts. |
| 12 | Starlight Wrath 3/5 | Faster casts. |
| 13 | Starlight Wrath 4/5 | Faster casts. |
| 14 | Starlight Wrath 5/5 | Wrath and Starfire each cast 0.5 seconds faster. |
| 15 | Moonglow 1/3 | Lower mana costs. |
| 16 | Moonglow 2/3 | Further mana savings. |
| 17 | Moonglow 3/3 | Completes the early mana-cost reduction. |
| 18 | Nature’s Majesty 1/2 | More critical-strike chance on relevant spells. |
| 19 | Nature’s Majesty 2/2 | Completes your first ten Balance points. |
| 20 | Nature’s Reach 1/2 | Extra range and reduced Balance threat. |

## Your companions

| Companion | Role |
|---|---|
| Darethia | Protection Warrior · holds the front line |
| Lunethra | Discipline Priest · keeps the party alive |
| Faelion | Marksmanship Hunter · ranged damage |
| Saerith | Combat Rogue · melee damage |
| Thaloren | Balance Druid · ranged damage and support |

A comfortable pull is one where Darethia keeps the enemies and Lunethra has room to heal. Add more only when that feels routine.
